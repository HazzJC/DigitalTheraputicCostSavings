CareLoop ROI Calculator

The ROI calculator centres around inpatient admission avoidance and resulting reduced bed days from implementing CareLoop across a CMHT population. The reductions are calculated based upon an RCT published in the Lancet Psychiatry which showed CareLoop when used alongside standard care in a CMHT setting reduced incidence of relapse by 50%.

CareLoop can also positively impact other key metrics including length of stay, readmission rates and ED attendance, as well as workforce and patient satisfaction. As part of building a business case we work with you, utilising trust specific data to model annual savings and ROI based upon your specific deployment and use case.

Average annual relapse rate

A systematic review and meta-analysis of 65 trials covering 6,493 patients found 27% of people with schizophrenia relapsed within one year while maintained on antipsychotic treatment, against 64% on placebo. The 25% applied here sits just below that pooled figure. Leucht et al., The Lancet, 2012 25%

An individual who has a psychosis or schizophrenia diagnosis has a 25% annual risk of relapse.

Average cost of a relapse

£25,852 is the mean in-patient cost per relapse admission in a UK study of hospital treatment and management in relapse of schizophrenia, of which over 97% was hospital cost and under 3% drug cost. Munro et al., The Psychiatrist, 2011 £25,852

This is a conservative figure factoring in costs of admission not associated broader costs. Hospital treatment and management in relapse of schizophrenia in the UK: associated costs, Munro et al 2011
