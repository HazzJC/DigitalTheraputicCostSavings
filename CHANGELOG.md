## [1.0.0] - 2026-09-19

### Added
- **CareLoop ROI Calculator**: Interactive economic model for NHS CMHT psychosis caseloads evaluating digital therapeutic adoption.
- **Financial Projections**: Estimates 1-year and 3-year contract net savings, implementation setup costs, and annual platform license fees.
- **Capacity Impact**: Quantifies inpatient bed days avoided and relapses prevented across trust populations.
- **Live Tool**: Deployed on GitHub Pages at [hazzjc.github.io/DigitalTheraputicCostSavings](https://hazzjc.github.io/DigitalTheraputicCostSavings/).
